Research agenda

From sparse signals
to useful worlds.

My work couples images, masks, and geometry so AI systems can reason about structure—not just appearance. I focus on methods that remain controllable, uncertainty-aware, and useful beyond the benchmark.

01

Generative Medical Intelligence

Multimodal diffusion models that transform sketches, text, and limited annotations into coherent 3D CT/MRI volumes and anatomical masks.

3D diffusionMultimodal learning
02

Geometry & Scientific Computing

Geometry-aware learning for surface reconstruction, simulation-ready modeling, and interpretable analysis of complex scientific structures.

Geometric learningScientific AI

Embodied Perception
